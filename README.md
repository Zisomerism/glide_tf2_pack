# Glide TF2 Community Vehicle Pack

### [Download this on the Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3680758477)

A [Glide](https://github.com/StyledStrike/gmod-glide/) pack made from TF2 vehicle models made by various TF2 and SFM community members, most of the original uploads can be found on [tf2maps.net](https://tf2maps.net/downloads/). I found most of the models from [this steam workshop collection](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2020143572) of models made by [Stefano](https://steamcommunity.com/id/steddy96). All I have done to the models is remake the collision meshes, removed animations that Glide doesn't need, adjusted model origins, and made the wheels separate models.
